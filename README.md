# On The Go

On The Go downloads Instagram Reels ahead of time so you can watch them anywhere, even with no signal.

Log into Instagram once inside the app. While you're on wifi, it quietly fills a "tank" of Reels on your phone. On a plane, underground or out of data, you just open it and swipe. It looks and works like Instagram, so there's nothing new to learn.

## What it does

**Reels, offline**
The app keeps Instagram open in the background, scrolls your Reels feed with the sound off, and saves each clip with its caption, likes and comments. You set how much space it can use.

**Feels like Instagram**
Swipe up for the next one. Tap to mute, hold to pause, double tap to like. Hold near either side to speed up, and pull down while holding to lock the speed. A thin line at the bottom shows how far in you are.

**Learns what you like**
Watching to the end, rewatching, liking and saving push a creator up. Skipping fast pushes them down. Creators you keep skipping stop being downloaded. You can see and reset this in Settings.

**Saves and albums**
Save a Reel and file it into albums, same as Instagram's collections. Liked and saved Reels are never deleted to make room.

**Smart storage**
When the tank is full, it can replace Reels you've already watched, oldest first. Unwatched, liked and saved ones stay.

**Choose the mix**
Pull from more than one place (your Reels feed, TikTok) and set how much of each you want.

**Messages**
Your Instagram chats, inside the app: send texts, photos and voice notes, double tap to like, swipe right to reply. Chats you've opened before load instantly, and you can get alerts for new messages.

**Notes**
The Notes row from the top of Instagram's inbox. Tap one to read it in full. Song notes play the song.

**Comments**
Read a Reel's comments offline and write your own.

**Post for real, or don't**
Off by default. When it's off, your likes, comments and saves stay on your phone and Instagram never sees them. Turn it on and they go to Instagram as you. TikTok always stays on your phone.

**Mute people**
Mute someone's posts, their comments, or both. "Your activity" lists every comment you've written and everyone you've muted.

## Privacy

No accounts. No On The Go servers. Your Reels, saves, likes, comments and chats stay on your phone.

You log into Instagram yourself, in Instagram's own page inside the app. The app never sees, stores or sends your password. It only checks that you're logged in.

The app talks to:
- **Instagram** (and **TikTok** if you add it), as you, the same way the website does.
- **fysh.site** and **GitHub**, to check for updates. Nothing about you is sent.

No data collection. No telemetry. No analytics. No crash reporters. Phone backups are off, so your data doesn't get copied into a cloud backup.

## Setup

1. Download the APK above and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.
2. Open the app and log into Instagram.
3. Allow what setup asks for:
   - **Display over other apps**, so Instagram can keep running in the background while the screen is off.
   - **Notifications**, so you can see when it's collecting and get chat alerts.
   - **Microphone**, only if you send voice notes.
4. Leave it on wifi for a bit. The tank fills up on its own.

Needs Android 10 or newer.

## Updates

The app checks for updates about once a day. When one is out, a button shows at the top of the home screen. Tap it to install. You can also check in **Settings → Check for updates**.

Each update is checked against its fingerprint before it installs, so a damaged or swapped download won't install.

You can also add this repository to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) if you'd rather it update in the background.
